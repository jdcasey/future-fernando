# --- breath-based ---
Box breathing — In 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Repeat 4 rounds.
4-7-8 breath — In through the nose 4, hold 7, out through the mouth 8. Repeat 4 times.
Physiological sigh — Two nose inhales (second one short, stacked on the first), then one long slow mouth exhale. 3 times.
Coherent breathing — Slow, equal in and out, about 5-6 seconds each, for a minute or two.
Belly breathing — Hand on your stomach; breathe so only your belly rises, not your chest. 6 slow breaths.

# --- sensory grounding ---
5-4-3-2-1 — Name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
3-3-3 — Name 3 things you see, 3 you hear, then move 3 body parts.
Temperature shift — Run cold water over your hands or splash your face; notice the change.
Orienting — Slowly turn your head and look around, letting your eyes land wherever they want.

# --- body-based ---
Feet on floor — Press both feet flat, feel the ground push back, notice the contact for 30 seconds.
Progressive release — Tense one muscle group hard for 5 seconds, then let go. Work up the body.
Butterfly hug — Cross your arms over your chest and alternate gentle taps on each shoulder.
Eye-hold reset — Head still, look as far to one side as is comfortable and hold until you sigh, yawn, or swallow; return to center, then repeat on the other side.

# --- movement / in-office ---
# Quick bodyweight moves — no equipment, minimal space. Trim the ones that feel
# too conspicuous for your workspace.
Bodyweight squats — Feet shoulder-width, sit back and down, drive up through your heels. 10 reps.
Calf raises — Rise onto the balls of your feet, hold at the top, lower slowly. 15 reps.
Chair sit-to-stand — Stand up and sit down without using your hands. 10 reps.
Desk push-ups — Hands on the desk edge, body straight, lower your chest to it and push back. 10 reps.
Wall sit — Back flat against the wall, slide down to a seated position, hold. 20-30 seconds.
Standing march — March in place, knees high, arms swinging. 30 seconds.
Standing forward fold — Feet hip-width, hinge at the hips and let your arms and head hang. 20-30 seconds.
Hip flexor stretch — In a lunge or half-kneel, tuck your pelvis and open the front of the back hip. 20 seconds each side.
Doorway chest stretch — Forearm on the door frame, step through until the chest opens. 20 seconds each side.
Neck release — Ear toward one shoulder, let the opposite hand hang heavy. 20 seconds each side.
Shoulder rolls — Roll both shoulders back and down in slow circles. 10 rolls.
Torso twist — Feet planted, rotate your upper body side to side and let your arms swing. 10 twists.
Ankle circles — Lift one foot, draw slow circles with the toes both directions. 10 each side, then switch.
